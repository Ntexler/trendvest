# TrendVest — working agreement with Claude

## How we work (set by the project owner)
- **Competitor check first.** Before proposing any feature, check what already exists (competitors, open-source, academic research) and summarize it briefly with sources.
- **Be a sparring partner.** Challenge assumptions, point out weaknesses and risks, and brainstorm alternatives — don't just agree.
- **No building before approval.** Present a plan and wait for explicit approval before writing product code.
- **Sketch before every screen.** Before building any UI screen, propose a sketch/mockup and get approval.
- Communicate in Hebrew.

## Current direction (Nov 2026)
- Phase 1 is a **personal tool** (single user) — not a public product yet. Public/general release only if it proves itself.
- Research track: a "News Impact" index that learns historically how news/trend shocks move stocks
  (reaction size, speed, settle time, reversal), including **spillover along supply chains** across exchanges.
  Code: `research/news_impact/`.
